Brain Boost! Mini Brain Games FAQ

Answers to common questions about Brain Boost! Mini Brain Games, a free brain-training app with 26 mini games.

Q. Is it free?

Yes, it is free. All 26 mini games are free to play, and no sign-up is required.

Q. Are my records saved?

Yes. The top 10 records for each game are saved, so you can see yourself improving as you play.

You can also save a backup and restore your records when you switch to a new device.

Q. Who is it for?

Each round takes a few seconds to a few minutes, so it suits anyone who wants to use their brain in spare moments. For example:

  • students who want a quick mental warm-up
  • office workers who want to recharge their brain between tasks
  • older adults who want to keep their mind sharp

Q. What kinds of games are there?

There are 26 mini games that train skills such as math, memory, creative thinking, reflexes, persistence in thinking and imagination. For example:

  • arrange numbers to make 10, give change at the register
  • matching pairs, remember numbers that flash for an instant
  • spot the odd character among similar kanji
  • mazes with reversed controls, the hidden side of a die, one-stroke drawing
  • brake for sudden obstacles, stop the slot reels

See the game categories for more.

Q. Are there ads?

The free version shows ads. You can remove all ads with a one-time purchase from the settings screen.

The app is available free for Android on Google Play.
