Brain Boost! Mini Brain Games Privacy Policy

This policy describes how user information is handled in the app "Brain Boost! Mini Brain Games" ("the App").

This is an English translation of the original Japanese policy. If there is any discrepancy, the Japanese version prevails.

1. Game records

Game scores and settings are stored only on your device. They are never sent to the developer's servers. Deleting the App also deletes your records.

2. Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob, an advertising service. AdMob may use information such as your advertising ID, IP address and device type to serve ads. For details, see Google's advertising policies. You can turn off ad personalization in your device settings.

3. In-app purchases

The purchase to remove ads is processed through Google Play's billing system. The developer does not receive your payment information.

4. Sharing with third parties

Apart from the advertising service above, we do not provide user information to third parties except as required by law.

5. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as needed. Any changes will be posted on this page.
