Simple Time Card Privacy Policy

This policy describes how user information is handled in the apps "Simple Time Card" and "Simple Time Card (Host)" (together, "the Apps").

This is an English translation of the original Japanese policy. If there is any discrepancy, the Japanese version prevails.

1. Information we collect

To provide the service, the Apps collect the following information:

  • Date and time of each clock-in and clock-out
  • Location at the time of each punch (latitude and longitude from GPS, and the address derived from it)
  • Group and passcode information, and registration details entered by users such as names
  • Device information for serving ads (advertising ID, IP address, device type, etc.)

2. How we use information

  • To record and display attendance, and to let group managers check attendance status
  • To provide and maintain the Apps and fix bugs
  • To serve ads

3. Location information

Location is collected only when you clock in or out, and is used as a record of where you worked. The Apps do not continuously collect location in the background. You can allow or deny location access at any time in your device settings.

4. Storage and sharing

Punch data, location information and registration details are stored in Firebase, a cloud service provided by Google LLC.

Punch data and location recorded in the Client app can be viewed by the manager of the same group (the Host app user). We do not provide this information to any other third party except as required by law.

5. Advertising

The Apps use Google AdMob, an advertising service. AdMob may use device information such as your advertising ID to serve ads. For details, see Google's advertising policies. You can turn off ad personalization in your device settings.

6. Deleting your data

If you would like your registered data deleted, please contact us at the address below. We will confirm your request and delete it promptly.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as needed. Any changes will be posted on this page.
