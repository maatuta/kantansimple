Simple Time Card FAQ

Answers to common questions about Simple Time Card, a free time clock app.

Q. Is it free?

Yes, it's free. Both Simple Time Card (for staff) and Simple Time Card (Host) (for managers) are free to download on Google Play. The apps show ads.

Q. Can I manage time cards for multiple people, such as a team?

Yes, you can manage time cards for multiple people for free.

  • The manager creates a group in the Host app and issues a passcode.
  • Members join by entering that passcode in the Time Card app.
  • The manager can check everyone's punches in the Host app.

With the "Someone else is using this" feature, several people can also share a single device placed at a shop or office.

Q. Does it record location?

Yes. Each time you clock in or out, your location is recorded by GPS and the address is shown. Even for jobs with lots of field work or direct trips to client sites, you can see where each punch was made.

The manager of the same group can check the recorded location in the Host app. Location is only collected when you punch in or out, never continuously in the background. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Q. Do I need a PC to sign up?

No. Everything from creating a group and issuing a passcode to members joining, clocking in and checking history is done entirely in the smartphone apps. There is no PC sign-up or admin dashboard to set up.

Q. Can I use it for shifts that span two days?

Yes. It records shifts where clock-in and clock-out fall on different dates, such as night shifts or late-night work.

The apps are available free for Android on Google Play.
