PDF Editor & Reader: Easy Edit FAQ

Answers to common questions about PDF Editor & Reader: Easy Edit, an app for editing PDFs on your phone.

Q. Can I write vertical text?

Yes, vertical text is supported. When you add text, you can choose horizontal or vertical.

Note that rewriting vertical text that is already in the original PDF is not supported.

Q. Can I convert other formats to PDF?

You can convert images and text to PDF. Create PDFs from photos and other images, or from text files.

Q. After turning an image into a PDF, can I edit the text in the image?

Yes. OCR (a Premium feature) reads the text in the image, and you can rewrite the parts recognized as text.

Q. Can I export text read from an image as a text file?

Yes. You can copy the recognized text or export it as a text file.

Q. How much smaller does compression make my file?

The larger the original data, such as images, the bigger the effect. Files that are already small won't change much.
