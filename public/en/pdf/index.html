Features

Quick PDF fixes,
right on your phone.

From fixing text to signing and organizing pages — everything you need to edit PDFs, in one app.

Rewrite and add text

Select text already in a PDF to rewrite or delete it. Add new text horizontally or vertically.

Annotate by hand

Write notes and check marks freely with a pen or highlighter.

Shapes and photos

Add lines, rectangles, triangles, circles, arrows and photos to your PDF.

Signatures and stamps

Save your signatures and stamps, then place them with a single tap.

White out

Cover anything you want to hide with white. The text underneath is removed when you save.

Organize pages, create PDFs

Reorder, rotate, add and delete pages, merge another PDF and add page numbers. Create PDFs from photos or text files.

Text search

Search for text inside a PDF to find what you need quickly.

Password protection

Protect your PDFs with a password using AES-256 encryption.

Text presets

Save text you type often, like your name or address, as presets.
