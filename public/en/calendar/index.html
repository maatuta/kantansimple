Can I share it? Can I save reports as PDF? Here are answers to the questions we hear most.

Yes, with an invite code. 1 shared calendar for free, up to 5 with Premium.

Yes. Trips, overnight shifts and other multi-day events are supported.

1 on the free version, up to 5 with Premium.

Can I save reports as PDF?

Yes. Even the free version can save reports as PDF.

Yes. Attach images to events, reports and memos.

Yes. You can set notifications and alarms.
