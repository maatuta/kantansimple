Features

Everyday organizing,
made simple.

Calendar, to-dos and daily reports, together in one place so you can find what you need right away.

Easy-to-read calendar

Monthly view, recurring events, notifications and alarms, and image attachments.

Daily reports

Set your own fields such as income, expenses or hours worked. Monthly totals are calculated automatically and can be exported as PDF.

To-do list

Shows the days left until each deadline, and automatically tidies up overdue and completed tasks.

Share with family & teams

Send an invite code to share events in real time. You can stop sharing at any time.

Automatic holidays

Holidays for Japan, the US, the UK, China and South Korea. You can also add your own days off.

Notes & widget

Save notes with images, independent of dates. Home screen widget, available in 8 languages.
