Calendar Task List & Report FAQ

Answers to common questions about Calendar Task List & Report, a free calendar app.

Q. Can I share a calendar with family or a team?

Yes. Just send an invite code to share events with family or your team in real time. You can stop sharing at any time.

You can share 1 calendar on the free version, and up to 5 with Premium.

Q. Can events span more than one day?

Yes. You can set different start and end dates, so trips, business travel and overnight shifts can be entered just as they are.

Q. How many calendars can I create?

1 on the free version, and up to 5 with Premium. Keep separate calendars for work, family and more.

Q. Can I save reports as PDF?

Yes. Even the free version can save reports (daily reports) as PDF.

With Premium, you can also:

  • freely edit the PDF layout
  • choose a template and save reports for a date range all at once (PDF, CSV, XLSX)

Q. Can I attach images?

Yes. You can attach images to events, reports and memos — for example, photos from a job site, receipts or documents.

Q. Are there reminders?

Yes. You can set notifications and alarms for events so you never miss anything important.

The app is available free for Android on Google Play.
